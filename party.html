  1. Each participant must register before the event.
  2. Bring your own approved Pokémon team and be ready for battle.
  3. Respect every Trainer, Pokémon and event official.
  4. Follow the instructions of the Championship judges.

  6. The judges' decision will be final.

❓ Trainer FAQ

Who can participate?

Anyone who loves Pokémon and wants to test their Trainer skills can register, subject to the event rules.

Yes. Registration is free for this Championship invitation.

What should I bring?

Bring your Trainer ID, registration confirmation and anything required by the official tournament rules.

Who becomes the Best Trainer?

The Trainer who performs best across the qualifying rounds and Grand Final will be crowned Best Trainer 2026.
